# Framing numbers for guide scenes

Compiled 2026-11-10 from `research/reference_shots/` (RA = food_and_drink.md, RB = venue_and_people.md; sources
with URLs in `sources_*.md`). The shot-by-shot variants are in `skills/script-composer/references/shot_library.md`.
Values marked (judgement) are not stated by any reference: say so in the draft report when you rely on one.

## Composition on the table (single dish)

- One hero; one supporting object (cup, glass, napkin, cutlery) behind or to one side about a third of the frame from
  the hero, never in front of it; nothing on top of the hero [RA19] (placement judgement).
- Overhead: plate centred in the clear box, the cup at an upper third, cutlery beside the plate, the bottom 35% table
  texture [RA2][RA4] (positions judgement; the owner's revision 1 puts the cup at one o'clock and the fork on the left).
- 3/4 view: plate centre about 50% height, the far rim and a cup or glass behind it in the upper third [RA18][RA38].
- Orbit: a background with depth (wall, window, other tables) so the parallax shows [RA15][RA16].
- Personal plates at place settings, never the table centre (`STYLE.md`).

## Light cues to draw (yellow edges)

Window or side-back light for food, liquids and steam; back light against a dark background for steam and glass;
never front light for steam [RA9][RA10][RA18][RA42][RA43]. Draw the cue on the rim facing that light.
