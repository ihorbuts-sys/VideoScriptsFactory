# Shared venue kit and first complete visit

Priority: finish a useful small collection within the existing balance. The earlier 4,000-credit number describes a hypothetical full-library envelope; no new credit purchase is required for this pilot.

## Shared room, different venue identity

Use one entrance/window shell, two-person seating arrangement and public camera route. Assemble walls, floor, pavement, opening surrounds and the simple awning locally. Swap the service zone, menu graphics and a few key props for each venue; do not generate a whole building for every category.

| Phase | Guide content | Main kit |
|---|---|---|
| Arriving | Street-side 3/4 facade view -> shallow sideways approach -> same doorway opens -> continue inside. | glazed_door, window, signboard, local wall panels, local pavement |
| Visiting | Continue from the entrance; turn gently toward the window table, reveal two inward-facing chairs, then tilt between menu and actual table. | dining_table, dining_chair, window, menu_board, local saucer and plant |
| Tasting | At the same seat, arc around the cup at rim height, include the pastry beside it, then move into a readable close-up. Pour only after the spout-to-cup alignment is validated. | latte_cup, kettle, local saucer and plate, existing reconstructed croissant |
| Leaving | Return from the same table through the same public route, cross the same open door, then ease back outside for the final facade view. | glazed_door, window, dining_table, dining_chair, signboard, local pavement |

The tasting phase first teaches filming food and drink. A person actually eating or speaking needs the later character rig/hand/facial work. These sequences are planned, not claimed to be already rendered or published.

## Assembly and motion checks

- No plate at the centre of the table; use an actual place setting with nearby cutlery and a reachable cup.
- Chair fronts face the table; feet and prop bases use actual mesh contact points.
- Door swings into a clear area, opening stays walkable, camera remains on the public side.
- Walls faint or absent in ghost output unless their edge explains an entrance or reveal.
- Full-color and 65% ghost variants share scene, camera and timing.
- App viewfinder and level are separate from the rendered guide.

Before rendering entry/exit, verify the generated door can be split from its frame, its glass is transparent, and its opening clears the visitor route. Check actual mesh surface contacts for table, chairs and tabletop props. Grounding a model is not proof that its proportions or articulation are usable.

Keep one location through all four phases and create meaningful camera arcs, height changes and reveals. Do not fill the preview with unrelated next-shot footage or treat a static subject as permission for an unchanging camera.
