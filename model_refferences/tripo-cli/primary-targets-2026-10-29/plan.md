Candidate counts are keyword-based opportunities, not completed guide replacements. Each new object will retain its source GLB, reference views, native Blender model, six rendered projections, nominal dimensions, geometry diagnostics and alignment anchors. Food and tableware are independently placeable. A low-complexity geometry proxy is for later registration/rendering experiments; these generated clay models do not establish a scan, detector, or validated LiDAR registration pipeline.
