## Original character reference images

Six views for each of the three approved characters: front, back, anatomical left, anatomical right, top and bottom. The `creator/`, `barista/` and `friend_locs/` folders contain the 18 individual PNGs, original tool outputs, source scene images and exact prompts. Preserve the locs friend's accepted youthful appearance rather than changing his age between scenes.

The same neutral A-pose, clothing, hair and shared orthographic scale are requested in all views; generated images are not a measured geometric construction. Top and bottom were requested as vertical projections; the generated results remain illustrative high/low views with perspective approximation. Left/right are the character's anatomical sides. No environment, labels, lettering or UI are included in the images.

Lower legs, unbranded off-white shoes and the hidden apron-back straps/ties are coherent design completions because the supplied scene pictures do not expose every surface. The creator's pink bag stays on her right hip, with its strap over her left shoulder.

These are generated image references for modelling, not a rigged 3D mesh or a dimensionally certified CAD turnaround. `manifest.json` records exact prompts, inputs and output provenance.
