# Guide object library and reconstruction trials

The owner accepted the local image-driven workflow. Start with the
[new reconstructed model library](reconstructed.html) and the
[full-color and ghost scene gallery](../../catalog/clay-scenes/index.html).
There are 13 reusable prop assets, each with editable Blender files and six
model-rendered projections, plus six unapproved character studies. The connected
bar-to-table guide has 180 source frames at 15 fps and twelve seconds of playback;
its color and ghost versions are available through the development API. The
four-second coffee orbit remains a base-shot proof pending its human handover.

The pastry rack combines four reused reconstructed shelf/tray units, sixteen
reused reconstructed croissants and authored structural posts. Its food is
independently editable, supported and inside the extended trays. The failed
monolithic rack and storefront trials remain excluded from the usable gallery.

There are 27 generated design sheets, with six requested projections per
object: front, back, left, right, top and bottom. Each object's `design/`
directory holds the original generated sheet, cropped view files, exact prompt
and provenance. The images were produced using the built-in image generation
tool. Generated sheets are illustrative design guidance; their projections
are not measured geometric ground truth.

The owner rejected the first 16 parametric prop prototypes for insufficient
reference fidelity. The experimental barista rig is also rejected. Their files
remain available for traceability and must not replace the published guides.
The archived builders require `--prototype` to avoid accidentally resuming the
rejected method.

The local TripoSR experiment is an actual neural reconstruction from a saved
design image, with a retained original GLB/PLY, editable Blender file and six
model-rendered projections. Single-view geometry remains inferred, especially
on unseen sides. A successful export alone does not establish visual fidelity.

Pending work remains: complete reusable object and approved character models,
rebuild the remaining 177 scene guides including all 70 containing people or hands, validate contacts and
camera transitions, then export full-color and transparent ghost versions and
publish reviewed replacements through the existing API. The 304 approved
script-preview illustrations remain live and are separate from this rollout.
