# Soft 3D preview art direction

Premium stylized soft 3D illustration for Loca Camera, a friendly polished commercial clay/plastic rendering. Smooth matte and semi-matte materials, subtle roughness, rounded sculpted edges, gentle studio-quality lighting, soft ambient occlusion. Slightly oversized adult heads, simplified facial features, sculpted hair, rounded natural anatomy and expressive believable poses. Cohesive sunny yellow primary accents, muted sage green and teal, dusty pink, warm cream/off-white, charcoal and warm brown. Use yellow on a purposeful prop or clothing detail. Compact recognizable environmental scene with a few distinctive props, clear silhouette and focal subject, simplified depth-separated background. Match the supplied soft-3D reference in materials, character proportions and palette, never its collage layout. Single full-bleed portrait illustration, exactly 9:16, no borders or rounded card corners in the source image. No text, typography, pseudo-lettering, numerals, logos, watermarks, UI, viewfinder, arrows, pictured filming phone or camera, except when a handset/camera is explicitly the repair subject. Signs, menus, receipts and labels have only blank color fields or simple non-letter pictograms. No 2D editorial drawing, grainy paper texture, contour sketch, photorealistic skin or glossy toys. At most TWO visible hands in the entire image, including all partial/background hands; count them scene-wide. Each is anatomical, attached and natural. Crop all other hands out. All people are adults except children explicitly needed in the family scene; no alcohol with children. Beauty work remains schematic, with simple cosmetic shapes and no detailed lashes or skin. The camera viewpoint is physically accessible to ordinary visitors throughout: never behind a staff counter/till, at a kitchen/prep bench, or in a restricted workspace. Show staff activity across the real public counter boundary; do not invent special access or a public demonstration. Keep personal dishes at a usable off-centre place setting, fully supported, never at the dining table centre. Prioritize the exact instructional action, angle and starting framing over decoration.

Recurring visiting creator: friendly adult woman, warm medium-brown skin, dark-brown high ponytail with softly sculpted hair, simple dark eyes, sunflower-yellow casual top, sage trousers, small dusty-pink crossbody bag where framing allows. Use the same adult creator in selfie/reaction/visitor steps. Recurring staff character when required: friendly adult man with warm light-medium skin, short dark wavy sculpted hair, ivory shirt and sage apron with yellow straps for food venues; sage overshirt with a small yellow accent in other venues. When another role is needed, use a consistent adult woman with short dark curly hair and a sage top. Natural poses, no duplicated limbs. For three-person groups, crop all but at most two total hands out. Do not insert a person if the shot is object-only.
