# Sources

Every claim in `best_practices/*.md` and `commercial_shots.md` cites a number from this file; claims without one
are marked "(judgement)" where they are made. Three lists, by prefix:

- `F`: food and going-out kinds (coffee, bakery, dining, fast-food, entertainment)
- `N`: non-food kinds (beauty-salon, sport_health, shopping, services)
- `S`: commercial shot vocabulary (`commercial_shots.md`)

"Opened" means the page was fetched and read on 2026-10-24; "search" means only the search-result summary was
seen. Vendor and agency blogs sell something; their numbers are directional. Data fetched from Loca production
(categories, venues, menus) is described in `venue_kinds.md` and `menu_item_kinds.md`, not here.
