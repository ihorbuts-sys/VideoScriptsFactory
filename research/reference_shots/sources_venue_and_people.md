Status values:
- `opened`: the page was fetched and its text read.
- `opened (meta)`: a YouTube watch page was fetched. Only the public metadata was read: title, channel, upload
  date, description and chapter list. Transcripts could not be read, so a timestamp marks the chapter where
  the technique is shown, not a quote from the video.
- `search`: only the search-result summary was seen. Either the page failed (403, 500, DNS or paywall) or it
  was not fetched.

Vendor, agency and tool blogs sell something, so treat their numbers as directional. Platform ad research
(TikTok, Meta, Google) is about ads, not organic posts. Applying it to visitor reels is a judgement. Cross
references such as [S10] and [F19] point to `../sources.md` from the earlier pass.
