# Sources: food and drink reference shots (part A)

References for `food_and_drink.md`, collected 2026-11-10. Ids RA1 and up.

How to read the "Access" column:
- `opened`: the page was fetched and its text read.
- `opened (metadata)`: a YouTube watch page was fetched and its title, channel, date, description and chapter
  list were read. No transcript was available (YouTube captions were not reachable without circumventing bot
  checks, which was not attempted). Claims from these rows are limited to what the title, description and
  chapters state; the timestamp says where to look in the video.
- `opened (transcript)`: a third-party transcript page (rosetta.to) of the video was read.
- `search`: only the search-result summary was seen; the page refused the fetch (403, 410, 429) or its body was
  not retrieved.
- `carried`: entry from the earlier pass (`../sources.md`, S ids), re-used here under an RA id; its access state
  is the one recorded there.

Vendor blogs (FoodShot, Hohem, Absoluts, Detail, Vlogara, safe-zone tools) sell something; their numbers are
directional. Several FoodShot figures (decay times, melt times) carry no source of their own.
