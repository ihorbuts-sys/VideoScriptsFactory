# cafe-morning-ritual: Morning ritual

Kinds: coffee, bakery. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 4 steps, 22 s. Kept from the kit; all four step ids survive.

## Idea
A slow morning at a regular's table: the room in early light, the steaming cup, a pastry torn open, and the
creator at their corner saying what they order and how long they stay.

## Audience moment
Someone looking for a calm place for the start of the day, often watching the night before or over their own
breakfast. They decide whether this is a place to sit, not just to grab and go. It fits bakeries with seating
too, hence the second kind.

## Why this order
1. `light`: the room sells the mood; a pan is the motion that keeps the first second alive.
2. `cup`: the drink, with steam as the motion (steam-hold).
3. `pastry`: the pairing; most cafe orders pair a drink with food (best_practices/coffee.md).
4. `corner`: the human, in the creator's own words, naming the order and the time they spend there.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `light`: room-pan at eye level, wide, pan-right from the window across the room. Cut (wide to close is a
  two-size step).
- `cup`: steam-hold at cup height, close, static, then whip right.
- `pastry`: the whip lands on the plate; cut-reveal from high, close to detail push-in as the pastry tears. Cut.
- `corner`: pov onto the table, medium to wide pull-out as the creator leans back, face in the pip window.
Deliberate transitions: one whip (cup to pastry), the whip-between-details pattern.

## Changes from the kit version
- Total was 13 s; now 22 s: `light` 3 to 5, `cup` 3 to 5, `pastry` 3 to 5, `corner` 4 to 7.
- `corner` had a 7-word prompter with `pip: true`; now 12 words, 7 s at a calm pace.
- Directions now say how the phone moves (hips on the pan, elbows on the table for the steam, the lean back).
- No step dropped. A fifth step (the order at the counter) was considered and left out: the script is about
  sitting, and `cafe-first-visit` already covers the order.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the cup: stronger food hook, but the room in morning light is this script's subject.
- Ending on a selfie: the pov corner shot with pip keeps the "my seat" feeling and still lip-syncs.
- Note on the hook: a pan of a quiet room is slower than a pour; the 5 s pan gives the AI edit room to trim it
  to the 2 to 3 s the hook wants.
