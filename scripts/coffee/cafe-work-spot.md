# cafe-work-spot: Work spot test

Kinds: coffee. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 24 s. New.

## Idea
The laptop test: can you work here for a whole afternoon? The room, your table, outlets and wifi, and a score out of
ten with the best seat.

## Audience moment
Remote workers and students looking for a place to sit for the afternoon. They decide on seats, outlets, noise
and wifi, not on latte art (best_practices/coffee.md, "Laptop or study spot test" and "What first-time
visitors want to see": seating, outlets, light, quiet).

## Why this order
Follows shot order 4 of best_practices/coffee.md, with the room moved before the outlets so the chain runs wide,
medium, close instead of jumping from a detail to a wide:
1. `hook`: the question, spoken, states the theme in the first second.
2. `room`: tables, light and how busy it is.
3. `desk`: what fits on a table: laptop, drink, pastry.
4. `outlets`: the proof the viewer cannot see elsewhere.
5. `score`: the verdict and the one seat worth grabbing.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: static selfie at eye level, medium. Cut, camera flips.
- `room`: room-pan at eye level, wide, pan-right, ending in a whip.
- `desk`: the whip lands on the table; pov, medium to close push-in toward the cup. Cut (pov to high).
- `outlets`: high, close, tilt-down from the table edge to the outlet or the wifi card. Cut, camera flips.
- `score`: static selfie at eye level, medium.
Deliberate transitions: one whip.

## Alternatives considered
- Outlets right after the hook (the best-practice order): detail straight to the wide room is a jump the
  chaining rules forbid; the table in between bridges the sizes.
- Filming other people working: the room pan stays wide and the direction keeps it on tables and windows; no
  faces close up.
- A noise-level step: the room captions carry it; a sound check does not show on screen.
