# cafe-first-visit: First visit

Kinds: coffee. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 25 s. Kept from the kit; all five step ids survive.

## Idea
The first time at a cafe, told in the order a viewer needs it: the pour as the hook, then the door, the order,
the first sip and a verdict with the price and the street.

## Audience moment
A morning scroller near the cafe, deciding where to get coffee on the way to work. They want to see the drink
made, hear what to order as a first timer, and know the price and where it is before they walk over.

## Why this order
1. `pour`: open on motion, not on the door (best_practices/coffee.md, hooks: "open on motion").
2. `entrance`: the place, once the hook has earned the next 4 s; the name goes on screen here.
3. `counter`: the drink's name said aloud, with the menu board and the barista in frame (people on camera).
4. `taste`: the honest first sip is the proof.
5. `outro`: price and street in the last spoken line, as the best-practice file asks.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `pour`: pour-detail shot top-down, close to detail push-in on the latte art. Whip out.
- `entrance`: the whip lands on the door; eye-level walk-in, wide to medium (door-push-through without the
  push-through join, so the counter shows). Cut.
- `counter`: counter-over-shoulder held static at medium (the creator's own shoulder, so no staff consent is
  needed for the shoulder). Match-cut on the cup being handed over.
- `taste`: sip-reaction on the front camera, medium to close.
- `outro`: static arm's-length selfie, medium; stepping back from close keeps the cut from jumping.
Deliberate transitions: whip, match-cut.

## Changes from the kit version
- Total was 18 s; now 25 s. `pour` 3 to 4 s, `entrance` 3 to 4, `counter` 4 to 5, `taste` 4 to 6, `outro` 4 to 6.
- `pour` moved first: the old order opened on the sign, which the best-practice file rules out.
- `entrance` had a prompter with `pip: false`; it is now a plain "just film" step with captions.
- Prompters were 4 to 7 words; all spoken lines are now 11 or 12 words.
- `outro` was `shot: close` (the face close to the lens) with a "come for the vibe" line; it is now an
  arm's-length selfie that carries the price and the street, so the viewer gets facts, not a slogan.
- Overlays emptied, per the brief; the guides agent fills them.

## Alternatives considered
- Keep the door first: the classic visit order, but the first second would be a facade.
- A separate menu-board step: the counter step already shows the board behind the barista.
