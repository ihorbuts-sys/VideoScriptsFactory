# cafe-before-opening: Before opening

Kinds: coffee. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 25 s. New.

## Idea
Behind the bar before the doors open: the lights come on, the barista dials in the grinder, the first shot runs,
the first cup goes out, and the creator says what time they open and what it costs.

## Audience moment
Early risers and commuters who want to know when a cafe opens and whether the first hour is worth it. Barista
stories are one of the four coffee content pillars (best_practices/coffee.md, "Behind the bar").

## Why this order
1. `lights`: the dark room waking up states the theme at once: before anyone walks in.
2. `dial`: the craft, with the person doing it.
3. `shot`: the first espresso, the sound and the crema (keep the machine audio).
4. `door`: the doors open and the first cup leaves; the room comes alive.
5. `verdict`: the facts, opening time and price, in the creator's voice.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `lights`: static eye-level wide from the door while the lights come up and chairs come down. Cut.
- `dial`: counter-over-shoulder held static at medium (hands-at-work). Match-cut on the portafilter.
- `shot`: pour-detail at eye level, close to detail push-in until the crema fills the frame. Push-through.
- `door`: starts filled by the cup on the counter seen from above, pulls out to medium as a hand lifts it away.
  Cut, camera flips.
- `verdict`: static arm's-length selfie at eye level.
Deliberate transitions: match-cut, push-through.

## Consent and safety
- The barista is filmed from behind and at work; `lights` tells the creator to ask first.
- `door` films the first customer's hands and cup, not their face.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the first shot: a stronger motion hook, but the dark room is the only frame that says "before
  opening"; the AI edit can open on `shot` if the room shot is weak.
- A selfie with the barista: two people talking breaks the one-voice rule for spoken lines.
