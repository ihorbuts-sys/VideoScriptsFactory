# cafe-seasonal-drink: Seasonal drink

Kinds: coffee. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 26 s. New.

## Idea
The limited drink of the season: the topping landing, the build layer by layer, the first sip, the board with
its price and "until when", and a verdict.

## Audience moment
Regulars and nearby viewers who want to know what is new and whether to go before it is off the menu. Limited
items draw more interest (best_practices/coffee.md, "Seasonal drink"; bakery.md cites the same for launches).

## Why this order
Follows the "signature drink build" order from best_practices/coffee.md: the finished drink first, the build,
the sip, then the board with the price. A verdict selfie closes because a seasonal drink needs a "sweet or not"
answer that the board alone cannot give.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `finish`: top-down, close to detail push-in as the topping lands on the foam. Cut (top-down to eye level is a
  two-row angle change).
- `build`: pour-detail at eye level, close, static; the layers stack in the glass. Match-cut on the glass rising
  into the creator's hand.
- `sip`: sip-reaction on the front camera, medium to close push-in. Cut, camera flips.
- `board`: sign-tilt-down from a low angle, medium: the board with name and price, then down to the drink.
  Cut, camera flips.
- `verdict`: static selfie from slightly above, medium.
Deliberate transitions: one match-cut.

## Notes
- `build` is `shot: detail`, not `close`: `close` is reserved for the creator's own face.
- The board step is where the "until end of month" fact belongs; captions say it, they do not invent a date.

## Alternatives considered
- A push-through from `finish` into `build`: two foam frames in a row would read as one shot; the angle change
  on a cut is cleaner.
- Ending on the board: no human verdict; the payoff must match the hook (best_practices/coffee.md, avoid).
