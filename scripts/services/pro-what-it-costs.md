# pro-what-it-costs: What it costs

Kinds: services. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 27 s.

## Idea
A price list as a video: the three most asked jobs, the work behind each and its price, and how the
pro quotes, the question every first-time customer has.

## Audience moment
Someone who needs one of these jobs and wants to know the price before calling. They decide on
the price and how open the pro is about it.

## Why this order
1. `ask`: the question is the hook, said by the creator at the door.
2. `one`: service one, priced.
3. `two`: service two, joined by a whip so the list keeps its pace.
4. `three`: service three at a new angle so the list does not repeat.
5. `quote`: how the pro quotes, the trust step.
6. `book`: the booking line.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `ask` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `one` (whip-between-details): high, close to close, static, 3 s, out by whip.
- `two` (whip-between-details): high, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `three` (counter-over-shoulder): over-shoulder, medium to close, push-in, 4 s, out by cut.
- `quote` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `book` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Directions name the work, the tool and the result, with trade examples in brackets, so one script serves phone repair, cleaning, tutoring, music lessons and photography.
- No customer data in frame: phone lock screens, messages, invoices, addresses, family photos in a home; the creator's own device or room is used where possible.
- Prices are shown on screen next to each service; no price is invented in the lines.
- quote and book are both spoken, so their cut needs no size change.

## Alternatives considered
- The pro reading the prices: the kit lip-syncs the creator only.
- A photo session behind the scenes: fits photographers only; this script serves every trade.
