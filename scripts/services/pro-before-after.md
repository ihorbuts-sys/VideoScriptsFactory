# pro-before-after: Before and after

Kinds: services. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 25 s.

## Idea
The transformation: a real problem, the quote, the work shown not hidden, and the result from the same
angle as the problem. Works for repair and cleaning, where the result is visible.

## Audience moment
Someone with the same problem right now, searching for who can fix it nearby. They decide on the
price, the time and whether the work looks careful [N14].

## Why this order
1. `problem`: the problem close-up is the hook [N14].
2. `quote`: the quote: price and time, the two facts the viewer needs.
3. `tool`: the work from above, showing the process [N31][N32].
4. `detail`: a second, tighter cut of the work.
5. `result`: the result from the first shot's angle, the before/after.
6. `verdict`: the verdict against the quote.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `problem` (hero-push (high)): high, close to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.
- `quote` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `tool` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 4 s, out by match-cut.
- `detail` (counter-over-shoulder): over-shoulder, close to detail, push-in, 4 s, out by cut.
- `result` (before-after-match-cut): high, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `verdict` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Directions name the work, the tool and the result, with trade examples in brackets, so one script serves phone repair, cleaning, tutoring, music lessons and photography.
- No customer data in frame: phone lock screens, messages, invoices, addresses, family photos in a home; the creator's own device or room is used where possible.
- The result step repeats the problem's angle so the before/after reads across the cut; the first
  shot ends tighter, so it is a plain cut, not a match-cut.
- Under 30 s, as transformations should be [N14].

## Alternatives considered
- A time-lapse of the whole job: the kit records 2 to 8 s steps; two process cuts carry the work.
- The pro explaining the fix: covered by pro-meet-the-owner.
