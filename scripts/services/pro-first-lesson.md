# pro-first-lesson: First lesson

Kinds: services. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 26 s.

## Idea
An adult creator takes a first lesson [piano, maths, photography], so a parent or adult learner sees
how the teacher starts with a beginner and what a small win looks like.

## Audience moment
A parent choosing a tutor or teacher, or an adult wanting lessons. Teaching style and growing
confidence come across better on video than in text [N34][N35].

## Why this order
1. `room`: the room before the lesson sets the scene.
2. `teacher`: the teacher with the creator: the person they would book.
3. `hands`: the creator's hands trying, the beginner's view.
4. `correction`: the teacher's correction, the teaching style.
5. `win`: the first win [N35].
6. `schedule`: lesson price and times.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `room` (foreground-reveal): eye-level, close to wide, reveal-from-behind, 4 s, out by cut.
- `teacher` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `hands` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 4 s, out by match-cut.
- `correction` (counter-over-shoulder): over-shoulder, close to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.
- `win` (hands-at-work (pip)): high, close to close, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `schedule` (sign-tilt-down): low, medium to medium, tilt-down, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Directions name the work, the tool and the result, with trade examples in brackets, so one script serves phone repair, cleaning, tutoring, music lessons and photography.
- No customer data in frame: phone lock screens, messages, invoices, addresses, family photos in a home; the creator's own device or room is used where possible.
- The creator is the student; no child appears on camera (best_practices/services.md, What to avoid).
- Lines describe the lesson, never a promised result ('grades up in a month').

## Alternatives considered
- Filming a child's lesson: needs a parent's written consent and a child who cannot be identified;
  dropped for the adult creator as the student.
- The teacher explaining how a first lesson works: relayed by the creator in the selfie instead.
