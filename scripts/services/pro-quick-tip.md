# pro-quick-tip: Quick tip

Kinds: services. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 24 s.

## Idea
A tip the viewer can use today, shown with the pro's hands: the usual way, the right way, the creator
trying it. The venue is where more of that know-how lives.

## Audience moment
Someone scrolling for a tip, not shopping for a service. They save it, and the next time they need
the trade they remember the person [N14].

## Why this order
1. `hook`: the tip promise is the hook [N14].
2. `wrong`: the wrong way, the thing the viewer recognises.
3. `right`: the right way, joined by a whip so the comparison is one beat.
4. `try`: the creator tries it, proving it is easy.
5. `sign`: where to find the pro.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 6 s, out by cut.
- `wrong` (whip-between-details): top-down, close to close, static, 4 s, out by whip.
- `right` (whip-between-details): top-down, close to close, static, 4 s, out by cut.
- `try` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `sign` (sign-tilt-down): low, medium to medium, tilt-down, 4 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Directions name the work, the tool and the result, with trade examples in brackets, so one script serves phone repair, cleaning, tutoring, music lessons and photography.
- No customer data in frame: phone lock screens, messages, invoices, addresses, family photos in a home; the creator's own device or room is used where possible.
- Wrong and right share the top-down close framing on purpose; the whip between them is the change.
- 24 s, inside the 15 to 30 s that quick tips hold [N14].

## Alternatives considered
- Three tips: one tip fits the length and is easier to save.
- The pro saying the tip: the kit lip-syncs the creator only, so the creator relays it.
