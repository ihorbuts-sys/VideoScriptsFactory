# pro-meet-the-owner: Meet the owner

Kinds: services. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 25 s.

## Idea
People book a person: the owner, their hands on the work, one tip in their words and the starting
price. Fits any trade where the owner does the job.

## Audience moment
Someone choosing between local pros they have never met. They decide on the person and how they
explain things, then the price [N33].

## Why this order
1. `hands`: hands mid-task are the hook.
2. `bench`: the owner revealed at the bench, the place.
3. `hello`: the creator meets the owner, with the owner in frame by consent.
4. `craft`: the craft up close.
5. `tip`: one tip, relayed by the creator.
6. `prices`: the starting price and how to book [N14].

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hands` (hands-at-work): high, detail to detail, static, 3 s, out by pull-back-reveal.
- `bench` (pull-back-reveal): eye-level, detail to wide, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `hello` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `craft` (counter-over-shoulder): over-shoulder, medium to close, push-in, 4 s, out by cut.
- `tip` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `prices` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Directions name the work, the tool and the result, with trade examples in brackets, so one script serves phone repair, cleaning, tutoring, music lessons and photography.
- No customer data in frame: phone lock screens, messages, invoices, addresses, family photos in a home; the creator's own device or room is used where possible.
- B2B services with nothing to film use this script with the owner and a screen they control, with
  client names off the screen (best_practices/services.md).

## Alternatives considered
- The owner telling how they started: their words cannot be lip-synced to the creator; the tip is
  shorter and more useful to a viewer.
- A wide establishing shot first: moved second, hands at work hook better.
