# shop-owner-pick: Owner's pick

Kinds: shopping. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 27 s.

## Idea
The owner styles the creator: the owner's taste is what makes a small shop worth the trip, and the
viewer sees it in one piece, its price and the fit.

## Audience moment
Someone who prefers small shops but does not know this one. They decide on the owner's taste, the
help they would get, and the price [N25].

## Why this order
1. `ask`: the ask is the hook, a challenge the viewer wants to see answered.
2. `pull`: the owner's hand going straight to the piece shows they know the stock.
3. `why`: the reason, relayed by the creator.
4. `tag`: the price on the tag.
5. `try`: the piece worn.
6. `verdict`: the verdict.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `ask` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 6 s, out by cut.
- `pull` (counter-over-shoulder (static)): over-shoulder, medium to medium, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `why` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `tag` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `try` (mirror-reveal (pull-out)): eye-level, close to medium, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `verdict` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Every item shown gets its price, as a caption near the tag or said aloud; prices are the real tag prices.
- Other shoppers stay out of frame, and mirrors are checked for reflections before recording.
- The owner agrees to be filmed; the kit lip-syncs the creator only, so the creator relays the owner's words.
- The push-through from the tag fabric to the worn fabric hides the change of clothes.

## Alternatives considered
- The owner choosing three pieces: one keeps the reason and the price clear in 27 s.
- The owner speaking to camera: not lip-synced by the kit; pip with the creator speaking instead.
