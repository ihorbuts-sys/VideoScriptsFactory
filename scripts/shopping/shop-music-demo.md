# shop-music-demo: Music shop demo

Kinds: shopping. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 25 s.

## Idea
The product in use: in a music shop that is sound. Staff play first, the creator tries the same
instrument, a tip from staff, then the price.

## Audience moment
A beginner or returning player deciding where to buy. They decide on the staff's help, the range on
the wall and the price [N29][N30].

## Why this order
1. `chord`: the first chord, with sound in frame one [N29].
2. `player`: the player revealed from the hands.
3. `wall`: the wall shows the range.
4. `try`: the creator tries it, a beginner on camera.
5. `tip`: the staff tip, relayed.
6. `price`: the price of the one played.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `chord` (hands-at-work): high, detail to detail, static, 3 s, out by pull-back-reveal.
- `player` (pull-back-reveal): eye-level, detail to medium, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `wall` (rail-truck): pov, medium to medium, truck-right, 4 s, out by cut.
- `try` (hands-at-work (pip)): high, close to close, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `tip` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `price` (hands-at-work (push-in)): top-down, close to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Every item shown gets its price, as a caption near the tag or said aloud; prices are the real tag prices.
- Other shoppers stay out of frame, and mirrors are checked for reflections before recording.
- Shop speakers off while filming: background music can trigger audio matching; the staff play live.
- The only deliberate transition is the pull-back-reveal from the hands to the player.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the wall: sound is the hook in a music shop, so the chord goes first.
- Record shops and book shops: they fit the find-of-the-day and shop-tour scripts better.
