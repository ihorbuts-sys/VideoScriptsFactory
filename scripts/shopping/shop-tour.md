# shop-tour: Shop tour

Kinds: shopping. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 24 s.

## Idea
The tour for a shop with too much to show in one find: the scale of it, the main rail with its price
range, the corner regulars know, one find priced, and where to ask.

## Audience moment
Someone who has never been in and wonders what the shop stocks and what it costs. They decide on
the range, the price range and the help at the till.

## Why this order
1. `door`: the walk-in hook.
2. `inside`: the whole shop at once, the scale.
3. `rail`: the main rail and the price range.
4. `corner`: the corner nobody notices, the insider tip.
5. `find`: one find with its price, so the tour has a concrete item.
6. `till`: the till and the owner, how to get help.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `door` (door-push-through): pov, wide to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `inside` (facade-pull-back (inside)): eye-level, close to wide, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `rail` (rail-truck): eye-level, close to close, truck-right, 5 s, out by whip.
- `corner` (room-pan): eye-level, medium to medium, pan-right, 4 s, out by cut.
- `find` (hands-at-work): high, detail to detail, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `till` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Every item shown gets its price, as a caption near the tag or said aloud; prices are the real tag prices.
- Other shoppers stay out of frame, and mirrors are checked for reflections before recording.
- The inside pull-out is short (table to arm's length), never a backward walk.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening days spoken at the door: moved to the end, the hook must be movement.
- A pan for the main rail: a close truck shows the tags; the pan is kept for the corner.
