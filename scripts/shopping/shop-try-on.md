# shop-try-on: Try-on

Kinds: shopping. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 7 steps, 25 s.

## Idea
The try-on haul, kept to two pieces so each gets a price and a fit: fit and colour on a real body
is what a hanger cannot show [N26].

## Audience moment
Someone who might shop there this weekend. They decide on fit, colour, price and the range on the
rail.

## Why this order
1. `pick`: the pick off a full rail, mid-motion, is the hook.
2. `tag`: fabric and the price tag.
3. `curtain`: the walk into the changing room, the turn before the reveal.
4. `reveal`: the reveal: the curtain opens on the fit [N26].
5. `second`: the second piece, quicker, price on screen.
6. `verdict`: the choice in one line.
7. `sign`: the shop name and where it is.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `pick` (pov-pick): pov, medium to close, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.
- `tag` (hands-at-work): high, detail to detail, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `curtain` (door-push-through): eye-level, medium to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `reveal` (mirror-reveal): low, detail to medium, reveal-from-behind, 4 s, out by match-cut.
- `second` (before-after-match-cut): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 4 s, out by cut.
- `verdict` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `sign` (sign-tilt-down): low, medium to medium, tilt-down, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Every item shown gets its price, as a caption near the tag or said aloud; prices are the real tag prices.
- Other shoppers stay out of frame, and mirrors are checked for reflections before recording.
- Only the creator's own changing room is filmed; the propped phone faces the creator, not other cubicles.
- The curtain push-through hides the outfit change, the cut a try-on needs.

## Alternatives considered
- A mirror selfie as the reveal: the curtain on a propped phone reads as a reveal; the mirror is used
  for the second piece instead.
- Three pieces: two keep the price of each on screen long enough to read.
