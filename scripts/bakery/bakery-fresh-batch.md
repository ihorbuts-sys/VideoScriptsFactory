# bakery-fresh-batch: Fresh batch

Kinds: bakery. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 24 s. Kept from the kit; all four step ids survive, one added.

## Idea
From the oven to the case to your hands: the tray comes out, slides into the case, you pick one, tear it open
and say what you take home.

## Audience moment
Someone nearby in the morning or after lunch, deciding whether to stop. They want proof it is made there, the
case with prices, the inside of the pastry and when the batch comes out (best_practices/bakery.md, "What
first-time visitors want to see").

## Why this order
1. `oven`: fresh out of the oven with steam is a bakery hook (best_practices/bakery.md).
2. `shelf`: the case with labels and prices.
3. `pick`: the viewer's own moment, choosing one (pov-pick).
4. `break`: the inside is what sells a pastry; never show it only from the top.
5. `outro`: what the creator took and the price.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `oven`: eye-level, medium to close push-in on the tray and steam. Match-cut on the same tray in the case.
- `shelf`: rail-truck at shelf height, medium, truck-left along the labels. Cut (eye level to pov).
- `pick`: pov-pick, medium to close push-in as the tongs lift it. Cut.
- `break`: eye-level close, static; the pastry tears toward the lens. Cut, camera flips.
- `outro`: static selfie from slightly above, medium.
Deliberate transitions: one match-cut.

## Changes from the kit version
- Total was 13 s; now 24 s: `oven` 3 to 5, `shelf` 3 to 5, `break` 3 to 4, `outro` 4 to 6, plus `pick` (4 s).
- `pick` added so the story reaches the viewer's hands, as the summary promised ("to your hands"), and to bridge
  the case and the tear without a size jump.
- `shelf` was a wide pan; it is now a medium truck so labels and prices are readable (shot changed wide to
  medium).
- `outro` prompter was 8 words; now 12, with the price in the direction.
- `oven` needs the baker's okay; the direction says so.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the break: the tear is a stronger texture hook, but this script's promise is the fresh batch, and
  the tray with steam is that promise.
- A first-customer step: needs a stranger's face; the creator becomes the first customer instead.
