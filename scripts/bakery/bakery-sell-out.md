# bakery-sell-out: Before it sells out

Kinds: bakery. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 24 s. New.

## Idea
The sell-out run: arrive early for the item that is gone by mid-morning, show the line, the last tray, the
inside, and say whether it was worth the alarm and when to get there.

## Audience moment
Food fans who save posts for the weekend. They decide whether to set an alarm. "Sells out by" is useful
information when it is true (best_practices/bakery.md); one filmed bite can start a queue (the crookie, [F22]).

## Why this order
1. `hook`: "I heard these sell out fast" states the theme and the stakes in the first second.
2. `line`: the proof of demand.
3. `tray`: the few left, the payoff of coming early.
4. `break`: the inside, the reason people line up.
5. `verdict`: price and the time to arrive, the useful fact.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: static selfie at eye level, medium, the shopfront behind. Cut, camera flips.
- `line`: room-pan at eye level from behind the line, wide, pan-right, ending in a whip.
- `tray`: the whip lands on the case; high, medium to close push-in on the tray. Match-cut on the pastry.
- `break`: the same pastry at eye level, close, static, torn toward the lens. Cut, camera flips.
- `verdict`: static selfie from slightly above, medium.
Deliberate transitions: whip, match-cut.

## Consent
The line is filmed from behind, so no faces show; the direction says so.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the break: stronger texture, but the stakes (it sells out) are the hook here.
- A walk-in through the door: the line already places the viewer at the shop.
