# bakery-box: Build a box

Kinds: bakery. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 27 s. New.

## Idea
A mixed box on a budget: say the budget, look along the case, pick one at a time, open the box from above, then
the total and the favourite.

## Audience moment
Someone buying for an office, a party or a visit, deciding where twenty dollars goes furthest. Price per item
said aloud answers the value question 54% of diners search for (best_practices/bakery.md [F14]).

## Why this order
Shot order 3 of best_practices/bakery.md, "Box build", with the case added before the picks so the viewer sees
the prices first. The open box comes before the total so the number lands on something the viewer just saw.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: static selfie from slightly above, medium, the case behind. Cut, camera flips.
- `case`: rail-truck at shelf height, medium, truck-right along the labels. Cut (eye level to pov).
- `pick`: pov-pick, medium to close push-in, one pick per beat. Match-cut on the open box.
- `box`: flat-lay-reveal top-down, close to medium pull-out from the pastries to the whole box. Cut, camera
  flips.
- `total`: static selfie from slightly above, medium.
Deliberate transitions: one match-cut.

## Notes
- The flat-lay-reveal is marked phone hard; the pull-out is short (close to medium, not to wide) so it stays at
  table scale.
- "Twenty dollars" is the pattern from the best-practice file; the creator states their own budget.
- `hook` and `total` are both high-angle selfies so the cut from `hook` to `case` (both medium) changes angle.

## Alternatives considered
- Picking at 10 s as in the best-practice file: above the 8 s step limit; 6 s with the edit cutting on each drop.
- Ending on the box top-down: no total and no favourite, which is the payoff the hook promised.
