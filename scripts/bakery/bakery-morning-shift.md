# bakery-morning-shift: Morning shift

Kinds: bakery. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 25 s. New.

## Idea
The baker's hour before the doors open: the half-dark shop, hands on the dough, the first tray out, the case
filling, and the creator's first pick once the doors open.

## Audience moment
Viewers who like to see that things are made by hand, and early risers deciding when to come. The proof "it is
made here" matters: a bakery caught reselling bought-in pastries went viral for the wrong reason
(best_practices/bakery.md [F23]).

## Why this order
Shot order 2 of best_practices/bakery.md, "Morning before the doors open", kept almost as written: the dark
shop, the dough, the tray, the full case, the selfie. Only the lengths change (6 s dough became 5 s) to keep the
script near 25 s.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `lights`: static eye-level wide from the door, the baker at the bench. Cut (wide to close).
- `dough`: hands-at-work top-down, close, static. Cut (top-down to eye level).
- `oven`: eye-level, medium to detail push-in until the crust fills the frame. Push-through.
- `case`: starts filled by a loaf in the case, close to medium pull-out to the full case. Cut, camera flips.
- `first`: static selfie from slightly above, medium.
Deliberate transitions: one push-through.

## Differences from cafe-before-opening
Same "before the doors" idea, different proof: in a cafe the craft is the shot of espresso; in a bakery it is
the dough and the oven, and the payoff is a full case rather than a cup.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the dough: more motion in the first second, but the half-dark shop is what says "before opening".
  The AI edit can lead with `dough` if `lights` is too dark.
- A laminating close-up at 8 s: satisfying but long; 5 s keeps the pace.
