# restaurant-weekend-brunch: Weekend brunch

Kinds: dining. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 28 s. New.

## Idea
Weekend brunch as a table: the syrup or hollandaise pour, the full spread from above, the hours, the yolk
break, the sunny room, and what to order and when to come.

## Audience moment
Friday night or Saturday morning, a group chat deciding where to go. They want the spread (portion and
variety), when brunch is served, how long the wait is, and the one plate to get. Breakfast and brunch is the
review kind in 9 sampled venues (menu_item_kinds.md: Chilaquiles Plate, Migas, Benedicts, pancakes).

## Why these steps in this order
1. `pour`: food in motion as the hook.
2. `spread`: what we ordered, the proof-first opener of dining.md.
3. `hook`: the theme said out loud: weekend brunch, served until when.
4. `yolk`: the money shot of brunch, the yolk break or the cut stack.
5. `room`: the sunny room and the wait, the second reason to come.
6. `verdict`: the plate to get, when to arrive, the price.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `pour`: pour-detail. Eye-level, close, static; cut straight overhead (two angle rows).
- `spread`: flat-lay-reveal. Top-down, close to wide pull-out; cut to the selfie (camera flip).
- `hook`: eye-level selfie, medium, static; cut to the back camera.
- `yolk`: cut-reveal. High, close to detail push-in as the fork breaks in; pull-back-reveal.
- `room`: foreground-reveal from behind the creator's coffee cup: opens on the cup in detail, slides past it to
  the wide room; this is the "pull back" half of the reveal.
- `verdict`: high selfie, static.
Deliberate transitions: pull-back-reveal (1).

## Notes
- `spread` is phone hard (flat-lay-reveal needs a still lift while standing); the direction keeps it short and
  tells the creator to stand.

## Alternatives considered
- Spread first: a strong proof frame, but a pour moves and holds the first second better.
- A food-review-table variant: that script reviews dishes one by one; this one sells the brunch occasion.
- Starting with the hook selfie: pushes the food to second place; the hook comes after two food shots instead.
