# restaurant-date-night: Date night

Kinds: dining. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 26 s. Existing script, kept and reworked.

## Idea
A couple's evening out: the lit entrance at dusk, the room, the plate landing, the first bite, and what dinner
for two cost with one booking tip. The room is half of why people pick a date-night restaurant.

## Audience moment
Someone planning a Friday or an anniversary, scrolling in the afternoon. They decide on atmosphere (light, noise,
how full), one plate that looks worth it, the bill for two and whether they need to book (dining.md: "looked like a
fun place" 45%, value for money 54%).

## Why these steps in this order
1. `entrance`: dusk and the lit windows set the theme in the first 3 s; the push into the door carries us in.
2. `room`: the second reason to come, shown before the food so the plate lands in a place we already picture.
3. `dish`: the plate arriving, the moment the table has waited for.
4. `bite`: honest reaction on the front camera.
5. `outro`: price for two and when to book, the two facts this viewer acts on.

## Changes from the kit version
- All step ids kept. Totals rose from 18 s to 26 s; every step is now 4 to 6 s.
- Prompters were 6 and 7 words ("First bite. Let me tell you."); now 12 words each, within 8 to 14.
- `outro` stays `close` (the creator's face near the lens) and now carries the bill and the booking tip instead
  of "Visit us", which a creator does not say about someone else's venue.
- Captions "Ambience" and "Good food, good people" dropped as filler; captions now name candles, booths, the bill.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `entrance`: door-push-through. Eye-level, wide to detail as we walk to the door; push-through.
- `room`: foreground-reveal. Opens filled by a plant or the door frame (the push-through's other side) and slides
  past it to the wide room; cut.
- `dish`: orbit-hero. High, close, a small orbit-left with elbows on the table; match-cut on the fork lifting.
- `bite`: static eye-level selfie, medium; the fork carries the eye from the plate to the face.
- `outro`: high, close, static; both last steps are spoken, so the cut between selfies is allowed.
Deliberate transitions: push-through, match-cut (2).

## Alternatives considered
- A room-pan for `room` (the old "slow pan"): weaker after a push-through, which needs a filled start; the
  reveal from behind a plant gives that start and a stronger reveal.
- A toast to camera as the outro: fun, but it hides the price, which is what the viewer needs.
- Opening on the plate (hook-first rule): considered; for date night the theme is the evening, and the dusk
  entrance says it inside 3 s while the plate stays the payoff.
