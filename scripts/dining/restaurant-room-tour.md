# restaurant-room-tour: Where to sit

Kinds: dining. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 29 s. New.

## Idea
A tour of the room framed as a useful answer: which seat to ask for. Through the door, the main room, along the
bar, the best table, and a seat guide (booth for dates, bar for walk-ins).

## Audience moment
Someone who already wants to go and is about to book, or a group picking a venue. The room is the thing they
cannot judge from the menu: size, light, noise, booths or bar (dining.md: the room, date night or group table
[F13] [F24]). "Where to sit" turns a plain tour into a tip they save.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `hook`: the promise on camera ("every seat, ranked"); a tour without food needs a spoken hook.
2. `door`: we enter with the creator.
3. `room`: the main room revealed.
4. `bar`: the walk-in option.
5. `seat`: the payoff, the one table to ask for.
6. `verdict`: the seat guide, sitting at that table.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: eye-level selfie in the doorway, static; cut to the back camera.
- `door`: door-push-through. POV, wide to detail; push-through.
- `room`: foreground-reveal behind a pillar or plant, close to wide; ends in a whip right.
- `bar`: rail-truck at bar height. Low, medium, truck-right along the bar top; cut (low to high is two rows).
- `seat`: high, medium to close push-in over the place setting; cut.
- `verdict`: eye-level selfie at that table, static.
Deliberate transitions: push-through, whip (2).

## Notes
- A tour has no food hook; the hook is the spoken promise, which is why this is the one dining script that opens
  on a selfie.
- Diners must stay out of focus; the reveal and the truck work best on empty tables, so filming before the
  rush is easiest (advice for the creator, kept out of the step directions so they stay short).

## Alternatives considered
- A patio step: not every venue has one, and a sixth location pushes the length past 30 s; the patio is a
  caption on `weekend-brunch` instead.
- A room-pan for `room`: a pan after a push-through cannot start filled; the reveal can.
- Ranking with scores: makes the verdict long; the seat guide is one line.
