# restaurant-family-table: Family table

Kinds: dining (written for `food-and-drinks-family`, works for any dining venue that takes kids).
`featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 25 s. New.

## Idea
Proof that a restaurant works for a family: the kids plate, the table, the kid-friendly details (crayons, high
chairs, lidded cups, play corner), the parents' own plate, and what the whole family paid.

## Audience moment
A parent deciding where to eat on the weekend. They check things they cannot see on the menu: is there a kids
menu, high chairs, space for a stroller, and is the food good for the adults too (dining.md family table [F25]).
The family total is the number they compare.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `kids-plate`: food first (hook rule) and the specific promise: a real kids plate.
2. `table`: the family at the table shows it is calm and has room.
3. `setup`: the details parents look for, the reason this script exists.
4. `bite`: the parents' plate; "kid friendly" places often fail the adults, so this is the proof.
5. `total`: the family total and the venue.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `kids-plate`: hands-at-work variant. Top-down, close, static as the plate is set down; cut.
- `table`: room-pan at table scale. Eye-level wide, pan-right across the family; cut (close after wide is a
  two-size step).
- `setup`: high, close to detail push-in on the crayons or high chair tray, ending in a whip.
- `bite`: the selfie lands out of the whip; eye-level, medium, static.
- `total`: high selfie, static, the table behind. Two spoken steps in a row, allowed.
Deliberate transitions: whip (1). One is enough for a calm family video.

## Faces and consent
The script films the creator's own family only. `table` says "your own family"; children of other guests must
stay out of frame. Kids' faces are optional; the pan works over hands, plates and the backs of heads.

## Alternatives considered
- The best-practice order (table first): the wide table is weaker than a plate as the first frame.
- A selfie hook first ("Kid friendly and the food is actually good"): kept as a caption on `kids-plate`
  instead, so the opener stays food.
- A separate family kind: rejected in venue_kinds.md (5 venues); this script covers them inside dining.
