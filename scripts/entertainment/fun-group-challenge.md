# fun-group-challenge: Group challenge

Kinds: entertainment (laser tag, bowling, mini golf, go-karts, arcades, escape rooms). `featuresMenuItem: false`.
5 steps, 25 s. Activity family; covers the "group night" idea of the best-practice notes. New.

## Idea
Friends take on the game: the bet, POV inside the action, the reaction, the scoreboard, and the price per
player with a "tag your team" close.

## Audience moment
Someone organizing a birthday, a team outing or a Friday with friends. They decide on how fun it looks with a
group and the price per player. Tag-a-friend is the named share for this kind, so the close asks for it.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `hook`: a selfie with the whole group and a stake ("loser buys the snacks") opens on faces and a reason to
   watch to the end.
2. `action`: POV inside the game, the main attraction.
3. `reaction`: the group after the round, the human moment.
4. `score`: the payoff of the bet.
5. `verdict`: who won, price per player, where to book.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: selfie held slightly high so the group fits, medium, static. Cut (camera flip).
- `action`: pov, medium, pan-left turning with the action, ending in a whip left.
- `reaction`: enters on the whip, eye-level medium, static on the group. Cut.
- `score`: low angle under the board, detail on the winner's name pulling out to close on every score. Cut
  (camera flip).
- `verdict`: static selfie with the group. End card.
Deliberate transition: whip (1).

## Faces and safety
- Hook, reaction and verdict film only the creator's own group, and the direction says to film only friends
  who agreed. The POV step points at the game, not at other players' faces.
- "Where phones are allowed" in the action step: laser tag arenas and karting tracks often ban handheld
  phones; the creator then films from the viewing area.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the action (as in fun-first-try): the bet makes this one a story with an ending; it keeps the
  two activity scripts different.
- A team lineup in gear at a low angle: one more step of the same faces as the hook.
- Food and drinks after the game: belongs to the night-out scripts; this one stays on the activity.
