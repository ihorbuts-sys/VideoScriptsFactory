# bar-tasting: Tasting flight

Kinds: entertainment, dining (a pub or brewpub filed under dining). `featuresMenuItem: false`. 5 steps, 25 s.
Night-out family. Kept from the kit catalog (was `craft-beer`). Step ids kept: `flight`, `first`, `favorite`,
`outro`; one step added (`taps`).

## Idea
A flight of beers or tasters rated on camera: the row of glasses, where they come from, the first rating, the
winner, and where to order it.

## Audience moment
A brewery or taproom regular, or someone choosing a weekend afternoon spot. They decide from the tap list,
the styles, and whether a flight is a cheap way to try several. Covers the "brewery walk" shot order of the
best-practice notes in five steps.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `flight`: four glasses light to dark is the hook and says "tasting" without words.
2. `taps`: new. The tap board or the tanks answer "brewed here?" and show the list with prices.
3. `first`: the first rating. Small sip, one glass in hand.
4. `favorite`: the winner, said close to the lens; the glass raised into the lens sets up the reveal.
5. `outro`: the bar and the taps open up from the same glass, a place to end with the name.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `flight`: rail-truck at a high angle, close, truck-right along the rims. Cut.
- `taps`: low angle, medium, tilt-down from the board to the handles (sign-tilt-down pattern). Cut (camera flip).
- `first`: static selfie at arm's length, eye-level medium. Cut.
- `favorite`: close to the lens, push-in as the glass rises until it fills the frame. Pull-back-reveal.
- `outro`: high angle, starts on the glass detail on the bar and pulls out to medium on the bar and taps.
Deliberate transition: pull-back-reveal (1).

## Changes from the kit version
- `outro` was a medium back-camera step with a prompter, which the format does not allow for a just-film step;
  it is now just-film with three captions, and the name is said in `favorite` and the captions.
- Prompters were 5 to 8 words; now 12. Total was 14 s; now 25 s.
- `favorite` stays `shot: close` because it is the creator's face close to the lens.

## Alternatives considered
- A spoken rating per glass (four selfies): 40 s of talking heads; one rating plus the winner tells the story.
- Making `outro` a selfie verdict: three spoken steps in a row already; the reveal of the bar gives the edit
  a visual end.
- Rating out of ten on screen as a graphic: the AI edit can add it from the captions; the step stays simple.
