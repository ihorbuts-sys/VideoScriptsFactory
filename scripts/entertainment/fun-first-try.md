# fun-first-try: First try

Kinds: entertainment (waterparks, adventure parks, zipline, laser tag, go-karts). `featuresMenuItem: false`.
5 steps, 25 s. Activity family. New. Activity scripts in Going out use the `fun-` prefix
(bar and night-out scripts use `bar-` and `night-`).

## Idea
The best-practice "activity run": POV of the main attraction first, the reaction, how big the place is, the
ticket, and the price with how long the visit took.

## Audience moment
A parent or a group of friends planning a weekend day out. They decide on the main attraction, the price per
person and whether a visit fills the day. POV of one slide is the format that went furthest for this kind.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `ride`: the attraction mid-action is the hook; 6 s because motion holds attention.
2. `hook`: the reaction straight after, out of breath or soaked.
3. `park`: everything else there is, from above, so it is not a one-ride place.
4. `ticket`: the wristband or the price board, the fact a visitor needs.
5. `verdict`: price, how long we stayed, where.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `ride`: pov, wide to medium push-in as the ride moves forward. Cut (camera flip).
- `hook`: static selfie at eye level. Cut (camera flip).
- `park`: room-pan from a high point, wide, pan-right, ending in a whip.
- `ticket`: enters on the whip, eye-level, close to detail push-in onto the price. Cut (camera flip).
- `verdict`: static selfie with the entrance behind. End card.
Deliberate transition: whip (1).

## Safety and faces
- The ride step says "only where phones are allowed" and asks for a strap or a waterproof pouch: many parks
  ban loose phones on slides and coasters. If phones are not allowed, the creator films the start line or the
  slide exit from the side instead; the direction leaves that to the venue's rules.
- No safety briefing or near miss is played for laughs (best-practice notes).
- The park pan is wide from a high point, so other guests are small and unrecognizable.

## Alternatives considered
- A queue or height-limit step: useful, but a sixth step pushes past 30 s; the verdict line covers "how long".
- Opening on the selfie: the POV is the shareable draw; the face comes second.
- Orbit around the attraction: hard at room scale for one person, and the pan shows more of the park.
