# night-live-music: Live music night

Kinds: entertainment. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 30 s. Night-out family. New.

## Idea
The night arc from the best-practice notes: walk in to the music, say what tonight is, the band, a drink
between sets, the event board, and which night to come with the cover charge.

## Audience moment
Someone planning a weeknight or weekend out, sound on. They decide on three facts: what kind of music, which
night, and what it costs to get in. The board and the verdict answer them; the stage gives the reason.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `walkin`: POV with the music audible is the hook for this kind; it lasts 4 s so the long walk-in the notes
   warn against never happens.
2. `hook`: a face and a line inside 6 s; the stage lights behind say where we are.
3. `stage`: the room at its loudest, the main reason to come. 6 s because the band holds attention.
4. `drink`: a drink between sets, a single glass, shown from above.
5. `board`: "when matters as much as where" (best-practice notes): the schedule or the band list.
6. `verdict`: which night, the cover, where.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `walkin`: pov, wide to medium, push-in as the creator walks toward the stage. Cut (camera flip).
- `hook`: static selfie, eye-level medium. Cut (camera flip).
- `stage`: low angle from the table, wide to medium push-in toward the band, ending in a whip right.
- `drink`: enters on the whip, high angle over the glass on the bar, close, static while it is set down. Cut.
- `board`: low angle under the board, close to detail push-in on tonight's line. Cut (camera flip).
- `verdict`: static selfie. End card.
Deliberate transition: whip (1). Two push-ins are separated by static steps, so no three in a row.

## Faces and safety
The stage step asks for the band and the lights, with other guests out of close-up; a low angle from the
table puts the crowd as soft shoulders in the foreground, not faces.

## Alternatives considered
- A toast with friends (as in bar-night-out): repeats that script; the board is the fact this one adds.
- Filming the crowd singing: strangers' faces; the band and the lights carry the same energy.
- Opening on the stage: stronger visual, but the walk-in with sound is what makes the viewer feel they arrive;
  kept short at 4 s.
