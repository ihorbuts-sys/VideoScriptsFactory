# bar-happy-hour: Happy hour

Kinds: entertainment, dining (pubs and grills with a happy hour). `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 27 s.
Night-out family, the offer-or-event slot. New.

## Idea
What happy hour buys: the first pour, the hours and prices on the board, the creator saying it is on now, one
drink and one bite from above, the room, and the times and name to close.

## Audience moment
Someone finishing work at five, looking for somewhere cheap to sit for an hour. They decide on the hours, the
prices and whether there is food; the script shows all three before the 20 s mark.

## Why these steps in this order
1. `pour`: the pour hook, pushed in until the foam fills the frame.
2. `board`: the hours and prices straight after, opened out of the filled frame. The price board is the offer.
3. `hook`: a face and a line within 9 s; it frames the deal.
4. `bite`: one drink and one bite. The script names one of each on purpose: the deal, not a round of drinks.
5. `room`: the after-work crowd, wide, no close faces.
6. `verdict`: hours, what was paid, the name.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `pour`: pour-detail at eye level, close to detail push-in into the foam or ice. Push-through.
- `board`: opens filled by the board, low angle, detail to close pull-out until the prices show. Cut (camera flip).
- `hook`: static selfie. Cut (camera flip).
- `bite`: top-down medium, static, a hand reaches in for the glass (hands-at-work as the action). Cut.
- `room`: room-pan at eye level, wide, pan-left. Cut (camera flip).
- `verdict`: static selfie. End card.
Deliberate transition: push-through (1). The pour push-in and the board pull-out are joined by the
push-through, which the chaining rules allow for opposite moves.

## Notes
- No prices or hours are written into the lines: venues differ, and the creator reads them off the board.
- Overlaps `food-review-pour` only in the first shot; that script reviews one drink, this one sells the deal.

## Alternatives considered
- Hook selfie first: the pour is the stronger first second; the deal is said by 9 s.
- Two drinks to show the price: reads as drinking more because it is cheap, which the brief rules out.
- Ending on the board again: the verdict already says the hours; the face closes better.
