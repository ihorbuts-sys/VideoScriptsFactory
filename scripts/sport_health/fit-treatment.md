# fit-treatment: Treatment booking

Kinds: sport_health, beauty-salon. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 24 s.

## Idea
One booked treatment (massage, facial, an IV bar session) shown as the place and the person, with the
price on screen and a feeling at the end instead of any promised effect.

## Audience moment
Someone choosing a place for a first booking. They decide on how clean the room is, what it costs,
and whether the practitioner explains things.

## Why this order
1. `room`: the empty room is the hook and the cleanliness answer at once.
2. `menu`: the treatment and its price, early.
3. `walkthrough`: the walkthrough shows how the practitioner treats a first-timer.
4. `during`: hands or feet only during the session, from a propped phone.
5. `after`: a feeling line; no claim about skin, sleep, immunity or recovery [N20].
6. `sign`: the entrance and the booking prompt.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `room` (foreground-reveal): eye-level, close to medium, reveal-from-behind, 4 s, out by cut.
- `menu` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `walkthrough` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `during` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 3 s, out by match-cut.
- `after` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `sign` (facade-pull-back (short)): eye-level, close to medium, pull-out, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- Lines describe the session and how it felt; no body, health, detox or recovery claims.
- beauty-salon is a second kind because facials and massage are also booked at salons and spas.
- The match-cut from during to after is on the creator: hands at rest, then the face at rest.

## Alternatives considered
- Med-spa procedures (injectables, peels): out of scope, they need risk disclosure [N4].
- A shot of the practitioner's hands at work on the creator: needs a second person to hold the phone.
