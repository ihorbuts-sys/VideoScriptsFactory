# fit-coach-move: One move from the coach

Kinds: sport_health. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 25 s.

## Idea
A quick tip shaped around one coach and one move: the viewer learns something before being asked to
book, and meets the person who teaches it.

## Audience moment
Someone scrolling for a tip, not shopping for a gym yet. They save the move, and the coach's
manner decides whether they try the class [N14].

## Why this order
1. `demo`: the move at full speed hooks; movement in the first second [N24].
2. `coach`: the coach, introduced by the creator: people book a person.
3. `stance`: the setup up close is the actual tip.
4. `copy`: the creator copies it, showing a beginner can.
5. `verdict`: a feeling line, not a result.
6. `class`: the class it comes from and how to book.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `demo` (hero-push angle, held static): low, medium to medium, static, 3 s, out by match-cut.
- `coach` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, close to close, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `stance` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `copy` (propped static, no named pattern): low, wide to wide, static, 4 s, out by cut.
- `verdict` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `class` (hero-push (eye-level)): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- Lines describe the session and how it felt; no body, health, detox or recovery claims.
- The match-cut from demo to coach keeps the coach centered so the size change reads as one person.

## Alternatives considered
- The coach speaking the tip on camera: the kit lip-syncs the creator only, so the creator relays it.
- Three moves: one move fits 25 s and is easier to save and repeat.
