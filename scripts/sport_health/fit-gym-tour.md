# fit-gym-tour: Gym tour

Kinds: sport_health. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 24 s.

## Idea
The tour that a first-timer wants before signing up: one thing that makes this gym different, then the
floor, the kit, the changing room and the owner saying who trains here.

## Audience moment
Someone comparing gyms near home. They decide on space, kit, cleanliness of the changing room
and whether they will feel out of place [N23].

## Why this order
1. `kit`: the standout kit is the hook; a facade would lose the first second.
2. `floor`: the floor shows space; film in a quiet hour so nobody is in frame.
3. `racks`: the racks show the kit at a close size, changing the scale after the wide pan.
4. `changing`: the changing room, empty, answers the cleanliness question.
5. `owner`: the owner says who it is for, the line that removes the fear of not fitting in.
6. `outside`: where it is and the booking prompt.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `kit` (hero-push): low, medium to close, push-in, 3 s, out by whip.
- `floor` (room-pan): eye-level, wide to wide, pan-right, 4 s, out by cut.
- `racks` (rail-truck): eye-level, close to close, truck-right, 4 s, out by cut.
- `changing` (foreground-reveal): pov, close to medium, reveal-from-behind, 3 s, out by cut.
- `owner` (spoken pip, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `outside` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- The changing room is filmed only when empty, and the direction says to check first.
- The owner step is pip: the owner stays on the back camera, the creator speaks.

## Alternatives considered
- Facade or sign as the first shot (best_practices tour order): moved out, the hook needs movement or
  a surprise [N24]; the sign appears behind the creator in the last step instead.
- A pan of the changing room: a walk-in past the door frame reads better and keeps the lens at one angle.
