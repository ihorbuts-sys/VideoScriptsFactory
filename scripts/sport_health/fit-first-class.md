# fit-first-class: First class

Kinds: sport_health. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 7 steps, 28 s.

## Idea
A first-timer takes one class from the door to the verdict. The price board and the coach come first
so a viewer knows the cost and the person before seeing a single rep.

## Audience moment
Someone who has thought about trying this studio or gym and keeps putting it off. They decide on
three things: what it costs, who teaches, and whether a beginner gets through it.

## Why this order
1. `door`: the walk-in hook; movement in the first frame [N24].
2. `desk`: the drop-in price, on screen early because cost is the first question.
3. `coach`: the coach: people book a person; the selfie keeps the instructor's consent obvious.
4. `reps`: the class itself, only the creator in frame.
5. `grip`: a detail of the work, to vary size before the verdict.
6. `after`: the honest line; a feeling, never a result.
7. `book`: the schedule and the booking prompt [N14].

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `door` (door-push-through): pov, wide to detail, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `desk` (facade-pull-back (table scale)): eye-level, detail to medium, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `coach` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 5 s, out by cut.
- `reps` (propped static, no named pattern): low, wide to wide, static, 4 s, out by match-cut.
- `grip` (hands-at-work): high, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `after` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `book` (hero-push (eye-level)): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 3 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- Lines describe the session and how it felt; no body, health, detox or recovery claims.
- The reps step is propped on the floor because nobody holds a phone during a set.
- The match-cut from reps to grip cuts on the top of the rep, so the change of size reads as one motion.

## Alternatives considered
- Filming three moves of the class (best_practices order 1): cut to one wide and one detail so the
  creator films only themselves and the take count stays low.
- Instructor speaking the greeting: the kit lip-syncs one person, so the creator says it in the selfie.
