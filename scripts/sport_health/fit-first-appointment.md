# fit-first-appointment: First appointment

Kinds: sport_health. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 27 s.

## Idea
A first appointment at a dental or skin clinic told without a patient on camera: the door, the empty
waiting room, the clinician with the creator, one room explained, the price, the verdict.

## Audience moment
Someone who needs an appointment and is nervous about a new clinic. They decide on who will treat
them, whether things are explained, cleanliness, and the first-visit price [N18][N19].

## Why this order
1. `door`: the walk-in hook, the moment a nervous patient knows.
2. `waiting`: the waiting room, only when empty.
3. `meet`: the clinician beside the creator: the person, filmed with consent.
4. `tools`: one room or piece of kit explained, the trust step.
5. `price`: the first-visit price.
6. `verdict`: the verdict outside, away from other patients.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `door` (door-push-through): pov, wide to close, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `waiting` (foreground-reveal): eye-level, close to wide, reveal-from-behind, 3 s, out by cut.
- `meet` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `tools` (counter-over-shoulder (pip)): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 6 s, out by cut.
- `price` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `verdict` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): eye-level, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- Clinics: no patient, screen, record, room number or appointment list in frame; a general media
  release is not enough for patients [N18][N19].
- No line describes a diagnosis or a treatment result.

## Alternatives considered
- Filming the treatment itself: dropped, it needs a second person and risks patient data in frame.
- Clinician speaking to camera: the kit lip-syncs the creator only, so the creator relays it.
