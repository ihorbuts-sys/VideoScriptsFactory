# fit-sauna-session: First sauna session

Kinds: sport_health. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 24 s.

## Idea
A first-time sauna or cold plunge visit told as a how-to: what the room is like, what to bring and
how it felt, so a nervous first-timer knows what to expect.

## Audience moment
Someone curious but unsure of the etiquette. They decide once they know what to bring, what the
room looks like empty, and what one session costs.

## Why this order
1. `door`: the door opening into the hot room is the hook from the best-practice file [N24].
2. `room`: the empty room shows the space without anyone in it.
3. `bag`: what to bring is the practical answer first-timers search for.
4. `step-in`: the creator walking in, filmed on a propped phone.
5. `after`: how it felt: a feeling, not a claim (calm is fine; detox or recovery is not).
6. `price`: the session price closes the decision.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `door` (door-push-through): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 3 s, out by push-through.
- `room` (flat-lay-reveal (room scale)): high, close to wide, pull-out, 4 s, out by cut.
- `bag` (hands-at-work): top-down, close to close, static, 4 s, out by cut.
- `step-in` (propped static, no named pattern): low, wide to wide, static, 3 s, out by cut.
- `after` (spoken selfie, no named pattern): high, medium to medium, static, 6 s, out by cut.
- `price` (hero-push (eye-level)): eye-level, medium to close, push-in, 4 s, out by cut.

## Notes
- Members and patients stay out of frame: the creator films staff, empty rooms and themselves only (best_practices/sport_health.md, What to avoid).
- Lines describe the session and how it felt; no body, health, detox or recovery claims.
- No step films inside a hot sauna for long: phones overheat, so the room shot is taken before the
  session and the spoken line is said in the rest area (judgement).

## Alternatives considered
- A selfie inside the sauna during the session: dropped for the phone's heat limit and other bathers.
- A cold plunge step: venues differ; the step-in step covers either the sauna door or the plunge.
