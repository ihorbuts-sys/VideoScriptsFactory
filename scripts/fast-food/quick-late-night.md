# quick-late-night: Late night stop

Kinds: fast-food. `featuresMenuItem: false`. 6 steps, 27 s. New.

## Idea
The counter that is still open: the hook under the lit sign, the sign down to the door, the order on the
griddle, the finished order, the bite at the counter, and the storefront with the hours.

## Audience moment
Late evening, after a bar or a shift, someone asking "what is still open". The hours are the fact they act on
(fast-food.md: hours in one phrase), and a lit sign at night is the image that says "open".

## Why these steps in this order
1. `hook`: "midnight and still open" is the whole promise; at night a face reads better than food in low light.
2. `sign`: the sign proves it.
3. `griddle`: made fresh at midnight, with sound.
4. `order`: the finished order as the hero.
5. `bite`: the reaction.
6. `outside`: the storefront and the hours as the end card.

## Shot plan as a sequence
- `hook`: eye-level selfie under the sign, static; cut.
- `sign`: sign-tilt-down. Low, medium, tilt-down to the door; cut (low to high is two rows).
- `griddle`: steam-hold at the flat top. High, close, static; match-cut on the finished burger in the same spot.
- `order`: hero-push. Low, medium to close; cut to the selfie.
- `bite`: eye-level selfie, medium to close push-in; cut.
- `outside`: eye-level wide, static, the lit storefront.
Deliberate transitions: match-cut (1).

## Notes
- Low light: every back-camera step is static or a short move, and the griddle and counter are the brightest
  spots in the venue (commercial_shots.md section 6).
- `griddle` needs the cook's OK; the direction says to ask.
- The closing hours are captions, voiced later, so the creator records them after checking the door.

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on the griddle: strong sound, but "still open" is the news; the sign comes second to prove it.
- A facade-pull-back as the closer: phone hard (walking backward on a dark sidewalk); a static wide is safer.
