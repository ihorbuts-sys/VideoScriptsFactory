# salon-brows (Brows and lashes), 25 s, 5 steps

## Idea
Script idea 6 of the best-practice file: a brow or lash appointment told as a before and after in tight
eye-level close-ups in the same light, one process shot, the reveal, the price. The captions offer a lash-lift
variant of each spoken line.

## Audience moment
Someone comparing brow bars nearby. The change is small, so the honest same-light comparison is the whole point
[N9][N39]; then they want the service name, length and price.

## Why these steps, in this order
1. before (close, 5 s, spoken): the creator's own face close to the front camera, which is what `close` means.
2. tools (detail, 4 s): tint bowls and fresh brushes; shows hygiene without filming anyone [N12].
3. process (pov, 5 s): from the treatment bed looking up at the lamp and the artist's hands; asks them first.
4. reveal (close, 6 s, spoken): same spot, same light, same size as the before.
5. book (pip at the desk, 5 s, spoken): service, length, price.

## Shot plan
- before: eye-level close static, cut to top-down detail (two rows).
- tools: top-down detail static, a brush stirs the tint; cut to the pov (pov differs from every row).
- process: pov medium push-in toward the lamp and hands, ends with the artist's palm over the lens:
  push-through.
- reveal: starts filled by the creator's palm, reveal-from-behind as it slides off, close, matching the before.
- book: high medium static on the desk.
One deliberate transition (push-through, palm to palm).

## Alternatives considered
- Separate lash script: the steps are identical; a caption and prompter variant covers lashes.
- Mirror reveal: brows are too small in a mirror shot; the close front camera shows the change.
- Price on screen only: spoken price helps search transcription [N14].
No medical claims: lamination and tint are described as services, nothing about growth or skin.
