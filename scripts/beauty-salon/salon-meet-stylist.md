# salon-meet-stylist (Meet the stylist), 25 s, 5 steps

## Idea
Script idea 5 of the best-practice file, with the quick-tip idea (7) folded into one step: introduce the person
who does the work, show their hands at work, their signature look, one tip they give every client, and how to
book them.

## Audience moment
Someone who picks a salon by the person. First-time clients want to see who does the work talking for a line or
two [N9][N1]; a booking prompt closes it [N14]. The creator is a regular client, not the stylist, so one person
speaks: the creator.

## Why these steps, in this order
1. intro (pip, 5 s, spoken): the stylist at their station waves while the creator introduces them.
2. hands (medium, 5 s): over the stylist's shoulder at a client's hair; direction asks the client first and keeps their
   face out of frame.
3. signature (detail, 5 s): the finished hair, hair only.
4. tip (selfie, 5 s, spoken): the creator repeats the stylist's real tip with them in frame. The tip is a care
   tip, no health claims.
5. book (pip at the desk, 5 s, spoken): how to grab a slot with them on Loca.

## Shot plan
- intro: eye-level medium static; cut to over-shoulder (counts as a new angle).
- hands: counter-over-shoulder, medium push-in to close; match-cut on the same hair, now finished.
- signature: orbit-hero from high, close, orbit-right; cut (camera flips to the front camera).
- tip: eye-level medium static, two faces.
- book: high medium static; both neighbours spoken.
One deliberate transition (match-cut from the work to the result).

## Alternatives considered
- The stylist speaking their own line on the back camera: lip sync and the prompter belong to the creator; the
  stylist's words are repeated by the creator instead.
- A separate quick-tip script: too thin for 20 s on its own; folded in here.
- A first-visit booking script with the price at the desk: the transformation and brows scripts already end at
  the desk with a price.
