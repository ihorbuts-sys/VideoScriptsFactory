# salon-nail-set (Nail set), 25 s, 5 steps

## Idea
Shot order 4 of the best-practice file (nails two-shot, [N11]): one nail up close, the color choice, the tech at
work, the finished hand turning on a plain light background, then service, time and price. Covers nail studios
(beauty-nails alias).

## Audience moment
Someone choosing where to get a set done this week. They judge the finish up close and want the service name,
how long it takes and the price, which [N11] says one line on screen should carry.

## Why these steps, in this order
1. accent (detail, 3 s): a macro of one glossy nail is the hook [N11]; outcome first, then back to the start.
2. colors (medium, 4 s): the polish wall and the pick; gives the shop a face without filming other clients.
3. tech (pov, 6 s): from the chair, looking down at the brush. Ask the tech before filming their hands.
4. turns (detail, 6 s): the hand turns through three angles with the phone propped, about 2 s per angle; the
   source suggests 3 s per angle, the AI edit can trim.
5. verdict (selfie, 6 s, spoken): nails beside the face, service, time, price.

## Shot plan
- accent: top-down detail static, the finger tilts so light moves across it; cut to eye-level medium (two rows,
  two sizes).
- colors: rail-truck, eye-level medium, truck-right, ends on the picked bottle; match-cut to the same bottle.
- tech: hands-at-work in pov, close static; match-cut to the same hand finished (before-after-match-cut on the
  hand, angle changes pov to eye-level).
- turns: eye-level close static, the hand moves, not the phone; cut (camera flips).
- verdict: eye-level medium static.
Two deliberate transitions (two match-cuts).

## Alternatives considered
- Whip-between-details over two colors: brighter, but the truck along the wall reads as choosing.
- Ending on the tech talking: they would be the speaker, and a step speaks one person, the creator.
- Top-down pull-out from the accent to the whole hand (flat-lay-reveal): marked phone hard; the propped turn is
  steadier.
