# salon-quick-tour (Quick tour), 24 s, 5 steps

## Idea
A short walk-through for a salon the creator likes: the door, the room, three details, a hello at the desk, an
invite to book a first visit. No service is filmed, so no client has to be on camera.

## Audience moment
A nearby viewer who has never been inside and wants to know if the place is clean and friendly before booking.
They decide on the room and the people, then need the booking line.

## Why these steps, in this order
1. door (pov, 4 s): "come in with me" is the hook, and it sets the location.
2. room (medium, 4 s): stations and basins, the first thing a new client judges.
3. details (detail, 5 s): three things that show care: polish wall, tools sealed in pouches, basins. Hygiene
   ranks first among reasons clients pay more [N12].
4. host (pip, 6 s, spoken): the team at the desk; first-time clients want to see who does the work [N9][N1].
5. outro (selfie, 5 s, spoken): how to book a first appointment on Loca [N14].

## Shot plan
- door: door-push-through, pov, wide push-in to detail on the glass, push-through.
- room: foreground-reveal, eye-level, starts close on a blurred plant, reveal-from-behind to medium, cut to a
  high detail (two-size step).
- details: whip-between-details from high, detail to detail, ends in a whip to the right.
- host: starts mid-whip in the same direction and lands eye-level medium on the desk, then holds; cut.
- outro: selfie from slightly above (high medium static), so it differs in angle from the host shot; both are
  spoken.
Two deliberate transitions (push-through, whip).

## Reworked from the kit
Kept step ids door, details, host, outro; added room so the push-through at the door has an inside shot to open
on. Total raised from 14 s to 24 s; prompters raised to 10 and 11 words. `coffee` removed from venueKinds: the
cafe kind has its own tours built on the counter and the drink, and this script's details (polish wall, basins,
tools in pouches) do not fit a cafe.

## Alternatives considered
- A room-pan instead of the reveal: the transformation script already pans the room; the reveal varies it.
- Ending on a price: a tour shows no single service, so the line asks for a first appointment instead.
- Filming a stylist at work: needs a client's consent; left to salon-meet-stylist.
