# salon-transformation (Transformation), 26 s, 5 steps

## Idea
The default salon story from the best-practice file: before, process, reveal, then name, price and booking
(shot order 1, [N9][N14][N38]). The creator is the client in the chair.

## Audience moment
Someone scrolling who has been thinking about a color or a cut. They decide in the first seconds whether the
result is worth watching for, and at the end whether the price and the salon are worth a booking. Price and the
person doing the work are what first-time clients look for [N12].

## Why these steps, in this order
1. before (selfie, 5 s, spoken): the honest before is the hook and it promises the payoff. Filmed in the exact
   spot and light the after uses, because different light reads as fake [N9][N39].
2. space (wide, 4 s): one look at the room and a clean station; hygiene is the top reason clients pay more [N12].
3. work (detail, 6 s): one process take; the AI edit can cut it into 2 to 3 short glimpses, which matches the
   "each step once, 1 to 2 s" rule [N39] without asking for three separate takes.
4. reveal (selfie, 6 s, spoken): same chair, same light, same size as the before.
5. book (pip at the desk, 5 s, spoken): service, price and booking on Loca, as the end card wants.

## Shot plan
- before: eye-level, medium held static, cut (camera flips to the back camera).
- space: room-pan, eye-level wide, pan-right, cut to a high close (two-size and angle change).
- work: hands-at-work from high, close push-in to detail into the cape, push-through.
- reveal: the cape-over-the-lens push-through from commercial_shots.md: starts filled close, reveal-from-behind
  as the cape or palm slides off, ends medium, matching the before. Cut.
- book: high medium static on the desk; both neighbours are spoken, so the cut is allowed.
One deliberate transition (push-through), placed where the payoff lands.

## Reworked from the kit
Kept all four step ids (before, space, work, reveal). Lengths raised from 14 s to 26 s; prompters were 6 to 8
words and are now 11. The old reveal used `shot: close`; it is the creator at arm's length, so it is `selfie`.
Added `book` because every salon video needs the price and a booking prompt [N14][N12]. Old overlays dropped
(the brief requires empty overlays; the guides agent fills them).

## Alternatives considered
- Opening on a 0.5 s flash of the finished look [N9]: needs the creator to film out of order; left to AI Edit,
  which can lift a frame of the reveal.
- Mirror reveal over the shoulder: needs a second person to hold the phone; the selfie reveal keeps it solo.
- Three separate process steps: pushes the script to 7 steps and the creator to film while being worked on.
